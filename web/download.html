Get the app

Gym Badge on your phone

One link, then it lives on your home screen. It opens straight into your routine, remembers every set, and keeps working when the gym has no signal.

Scan or tap

Point your phone camera at the code, or tap the button. The app is served over HTTPS, so it installs like a normal app from your browser.

Open Gym Badge on this device

Already installed? Open it from your home screen. Nothing to download from a store, and no account needed to look around.

Add it to your home screen

iPhone

Safari

Open the link in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

Android

Chrome

Open the link, then use the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

Desktop

Chrome or Edge

Open the link and use the install icon in the address bar, or the Install app button in the footer.

Installed from your browser over HTTPS. Your data stays on your device unless you sign in, and it syncs across your devices when you do.

What you get once it is installed

  • My Routine — your own training week, built day by day, with the movements you actually do.
  • The Guides — every movement explained: purpose, muscles, equipment, step by step, breathing, the mistakes people make and easier versions.
  • My Workout Log — log a set and it hands you the number to beat next time.
  • My Plate Truth — photograph a plate and get carbs, protein, fat and fibre, in plain language.
  • My Trainer — browse verified coaches and book a session in person or online.
  • Gym Equipment — tap a machine and see if it is usable before you queue for it.

It works offline. Your logs, photos and routine are saved on the device and sync when you sign in.
