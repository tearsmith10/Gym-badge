Questions, answered

Is there a free plan?
Yes. Starter is free forever — My Routine setup, My Workout Log, the gym locator, bookings and streaks, with generous food scans and body projections while we test. No card required.
What exactly do I get with Premium (₦5,000)?
Unlimited logging and history, NFC / QR equipment tap with live status, unlimited AI food & diet scans, unlimited body goal projections, smart reminders, wearable sync, offline first sync, and priority support.
Can I pay in my own currency?
Yes. Use the currency selector above to see your local equivalent. Your membership is charged in your chosen currency at the live rate.
Can I pay with Pi Network or stablecoins?
Yes. We support Pi Network (PI) and USDT / USDC stablecoins alongside cards, bank transfer, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Crypto settles at the activation rate and converts to your plan currency.
How does corporate seat pricing work?
Buy a seat block (5, 10 or 20 to start). Assign seats to employees by email. Per seat pricing drops as you scale, and higher tiers unlock unlimited logging, food scans, equipment backend access and NFC kits. Scale to 50 / 100 / 500+ on Enterprise.
Can we connect our equipment maintenance system?
Yes — that's what powers Tap In. On Business and Enterprise, Gym Badge can read your maintenance backend (or you log it manually in our console) so members see real time machine status. Access is optional and revocable.
Is my data safe? Is photo upload compulsory?
Photo upload is never compulsory. When you do upload one, it's encrypted, confidential, and deletable at any time. See our Privacy Policy.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel in one tap from your account. You keep Premium until the end of the billing period, then drop to Starter — your data and history stay with you.
