Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · Applies to the Gym Badge website and mobile application.

Gym Badge ("we", "us") is built on a simple principle: your body and your data are yours. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the control you keep. It is written to meet GDPR, CCPA and other global privacy standards, wherever you train.

1. What we collect

  • Account data — name, email, username, country, age, height, weight, sex, and exercise frequency. Sex, weight and height are used only to tailor recommendations.
  • Training data — the exercises, sets, reps, durations and bookings you log.
  • Optional photos — a photo of yourself for body goal projections. Upload is never compulsory.
  • Payment data — handled by our processors (Stripe, Paystack, and our crypto gateway). We never see or store full card numbers.
  • Device data — NFC tag interactions, app version, and coarse location to find gyms near you.

2. Photos are optional, confidential and deletable

You must explicitly consent before uploading a photo. Photos are stored confidentially, used only to generate your body goal projection, and are never sold, shared with third parties, or used to identify you without your permission. You can delete your photos at any time from Settings, and deletion is completed within 30 days across backups.

3. How we use your data

  • To deliver the service: remember your last session, suggest what to beat, scan your food, project your goal.
  • To personalise recommendations using on device and encrypted processing where possible.
  • To process payments and prevent fraud.
  • To improve the product in aggregate — never to identify you again.

4. What we never do

  • We never sell your personal data.
  • We never share identifiable health or photo data with advertisers.
  • We never make photo upload a condition of using core features.

5. Sharing

We share only with: payment processors (to take payment), cloud hosting (under encryption and confidentiality terms), and, if you're a gym member, your gym sees attendance — never your photos, body metrics or food logs. We never sell data to third parties.

6. Your rights

Depending on your region, you can access, correct, export, restrict, object to processing, or delete your data, and withdraw consent at any time. Exercise any of these from Settings → Privacy, or by contacting us. We respond within 30 days.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is restricted and logged. No system is perfectly secure, but we hold ourselves to banking grade standards and will notify you and regulators of any breach as required by law.

8. Children

Gym Badge is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We delete such accounts on discovery.

9. International transfers

We operate worldwide. Where data crosses borders, we use approved safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@gymbadge.app. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
