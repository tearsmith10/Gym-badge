# Photograph credits

Every photograph in the Gym Badge app, with where it came from and the
licence it is used under.

Three rules were followed when the pictures were chosen:

1. **A photograph shows the thing it is labelled as.** No picture of one
   movement is used for a different movement, and no picture of one
   machine is used for another. Where we have no honest photograph of
   something, the app says so instead of borrowing one.
2. **A photograph is used once.** The same picture never appears on two
   different movements, two different machines, or two different foods.
   A machine and the exercise that uses it may share a photograph — it is
   the same machine, photographed once, shown in both places.
3. **Every picture is licensed for commercial use.** Wikimedia Commons
   files are used under their free licences (CC0, CC BY or public
   domain); Pexels photographs are used under the Pexels licence. The
   Figma board photographs are the project's own assets.
